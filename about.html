ABOUT MERIDIAN

Built for the work.
Not just the vision.

Energy development begins with a clear view of what it takes to evaluate, drill, complete and operate a well.

OUR STORY

A Texas company with an onshore focus.

Meridian Oil & Gas, LLC is a Texas-based company focused on coordinating and supporting oil and gas development programs.

Our recent work in Louisiana involves technical planning, site and surface infrastructure, drilling coordination, completion readiness and operational reporting. We approach development as a sequence of measurable steps—not as a promise about production or financial performance.

We believe useful communication explains not only what has been accomplished but also what remains subject to permitting, testing, field conditions and commercial results.

WHAT GUIDES US

Three operating principles.

01

Technical discipline

Base development decisions on geology, well data, engineering assessment and practical field feasibility.

02

Operational accountability

Track what is built, permitted, tested and commissioned while identifying execution dependencies.

03

Clear communication

Distinguish completed milestones from future objectives, and make room for operational uncertainty.
