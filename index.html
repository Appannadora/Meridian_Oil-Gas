WHO WE ARE

A practical approach to American energy.

Energy development demands more than a compelling prospect. It requires geology, engineering, planning and disciplined on-the-ground execution.

Based in Texas, Meridian supports onshore oil and gas development with an emphasis on the decisions, infrastructure and field work that bring wells toward commercial operation. Our recent activity has centered on the Shirley Wells area in LaSalle Parish, Louisiana.
