A November 7 field report outlined drilling progress and infrastructure preparation across the Louisiana well program in LaSalle Parish.

Third well drilling progress

The third Louisiana well had reached approximately 4,000 feet, with drilling continuing toward a planned depth of approximately 7,500 feet. This was a progress report, not a declaration that the target depth had been reached.

02 / UNDERSTANDING THE PROCESS

Progress happens
one stage at a time.

Different operational milestones describe different stages of development. Understanding the distinction matters when reading field reports.

01
01

Drilling progress

Work taking place as a well is drilled toward its planned depth.

02
02

Geologic indication

Technical observations that require further evaluation and interpretation.

03
03

Well completion

Separate operations and equipment work required to prepare a well for its intended use.

04
04

Commercial production

A distinct stage involving verified production and commercial activity.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Drilling progress, a favorable geologic indication, a completed well and commercial oil sales are not interchangeable milestones.
