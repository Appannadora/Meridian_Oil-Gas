THE MERIDIAN APPROACH

Discipline at every depth.

A methodical view of onshore oil and gas development—before, during and after drilling.

HOW WE THINK ABOUT DEVELOPMENT

Good decisions are built
on good questions.

01 / GEOLOGY

What do the rocks tell us?

Review subsurface maps, offset logs, production history and formation characteristics. Identify uncertainty and the evidence needed for a drilling decision.

02 / SITE

Can we build and access the location?

Consider surface conditions, roads, drainage, pad construction, permits, contractor availability and field readiness before mobilization.

03 / WELL

What must be proven during drilling?

Drilling, logging, testing and evaluation provide evidence for completion decisions. Not every drilled interval is commercially productive.

04 / OPERATIONS

What does commercial readiness require?

Power, artificial lift, storage, separation, measurement, water handling, safety checks and purchaser arrangements may all be required.

05 / STEWARDSHIP

How is performance monitored over time?

Track actual volumes, water production, uptime, repairs, costs, regulatory obligations and end-of-life restoration responsibilities.
