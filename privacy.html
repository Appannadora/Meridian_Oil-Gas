SITE POLICIES

Privacy & email preferences.

Draft policy for review before launch · Updated November 2026. This page describes the intended handling of website contact and newsletter data. It must be updated when hosting, email services, retention periods and actual practices are finalized.

Information you choose to provide

Meridian Oil & Gas, LLC may receive your name, email address, organization, inquiry topic and message when you submit a contact form. A newsletter subscription collects your email address and consent. We do not ask for immigration records, investment account details, financial documents or sensitive identification through these public forms.

Why we use the information

We intend to respond to business inquiries and, only if you expressly opt in, send general company news, energy education and dated operational notes. These emails are not offers of securities or invitations to subscribe to a private placement.

How information is stored

The initial implementation is configured for Netlify Forms. Submissions may be stored in the hosting provider's forms dashboard and delivered to the authorized corporate inbox through email notifications. A separate email marketing provider may be integrated after configuration; the final provider and storage details will be reflected here before live subscription campaigns begin.

Your preferences

You may request correction or deletion of your contact information by writing to sales@meridianoilandgas.com. Newsletter recipients will be provided an unsubscribe mechanism when email campaigns are activated.

Service providers and website analytics

Service providers may process information to operate the site and communications. We will disclose applicable cookies, analytics tools and provider details before activating those services.

International inquiries

If you contact us from outside the United States, your information may be processed or stored in the United States or other locations used by our service providers. Additional jurisdiction-specific notices may apply.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to sales@meridianoilandgas.com.
